## Brandon Ratcliff Event

During the Country Music Awards in Nashville, Stay Alfred hosted a private, rooftop concert featuring Brandon Ratcliff. In the weeks leading up to the event, guests were invited via email and asked to RSVP via a dedicated landing page. I designed the HubSpot-based emails and RSVP page, event signage, VIP badges, and more.
