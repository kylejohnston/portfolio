The Grocery Buddy app is the best way to compare prices on products at your favorite stores. With real-time prices contributed by nearby shoppers and a smart shopping list with deal alerts, Grocery Buddy shoppers know they're getting the best price on everything they buy.

**My role:** Concept & Design

<Spacer />

## The Problem

Before Grocery Buddy, people who shopped at multiple stores were in the dark about where to find the lowest prices for the groceries they buy every day. Store websites and apps are hard to navigate and don't always show prices.

## The Solution

Grocery Buddy helps shoppers quickly find current prices at their favorite stores. Even better, Grocery Buddy's smart shopping list shows price changes and sales, helping shoppers easily save time and money.
