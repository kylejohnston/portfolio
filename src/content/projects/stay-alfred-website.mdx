## Partner Page Redesign

The primary goal of this project was to drive interest in Stay Alfred's partnership program, measured in leads generated through the HubSpot-based contact form. Improvements included an updated content hierarchy, more prominent contact CTAs, a dedicated contact page, and a dedicated resources page featuring case studies, webinars, and other lead-generating content.

**Built with:** Kentico (Headless CMS), React, TypeScript

## Company Page Redesign

The goal of this redesign was to create a visually compelling, easily maintainable page. Prior to the redesign, the page content was hard-coded, making edits nearly impossible. Now, the page is easy to update via a headless CMS. The redesign also included accessibility improvements and better integration of key company information.

**Built with:** Kentico (Headless CMS), React, TypeScript
